Privacy Policy for SecureSets

Last Updated: October 10, 2026

Introduction

SecureSets ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles your information.

Data Collection

SecureSets has no workout account or server. Your training records stay on your device unless you choose to export or share a backup.

The app also connects to third-party services for advertising, Google Play purchases, app updates, and (only if you choose) downloading the offline workout model. Those providers may process service and device information as described below.

What data stays on your device:

  • Workout routines, custom exercises, training goals, training days, available equipment, session length, excluded workout splits and completed or unfinished sessions
  • Optional body profile details: body weight, height and gender
  • App preferences such as theme and weight units
  • Purchase access cached by SecureSets and refreshed from Google Play

Body weight is used locally to show Pro lift-to-bodyweight ratios. Height and gender are profile details and do not currently affect workout generation or a strength score. These details are not sent to AdMob, Qwen, Hugging Face or SecureSets servers.

What SecureSets does not collect directly:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics
  • Device identifiers for our own analytics
  • Crash reports for our own services
  • Your workout data on our servers

Data Storage

Training data is stored locally on your device using:

  • SQLite - For routines, exercises and workout history
  • AsyncStorage - For preferences, body profile details, unfinished session drafts and locally cached purchase access

SecureSets does not add a separate database-encryption layer. Protection of app data at rest depends on the device, operating system and backup settings. A backup exported from Settings can contain training records, custom exercises, unfinished workout drafts, body profile details, theme and weight units; the destination app or recipient handles that file under its own terms.

Data Sharing

Your workout data is not shared with SecureSets or third parties by the app.

SecureSets does not operate a backend for storing your workout data. However, Google AdMob is integrated to serve advertisements and may receive device or app signals needed for advertising, fraud prevention, measurement, and performance. Where required, the app requests consent through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can change available ad choices from the app's Data & Privacy settings.

SecureSets does not send workout data to:

  • Our servers (we don't have any)
  • Third-party analytics services
  • Cloud backup services (unless you enable device-level backups)

Note: Google processes information under its own privacy terms. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Third-Party Services

SecureSets integrates with the following third-party services:

Google AdMob

  • Purpose: Display advertisements to support the app
  • Information: Google may process device or advertising identifiers, app interactions, app information, performance and diagnostics data for advertising, fraud prevention, measurement or service operation, depending on consent, device settings and Google's policies.
  • Placement: A full-screen ad may be considered after saving a routine, after leaving a completed-workout summary, or after every third 1RM calculation. A two-minute cooldown applies; ads are skipped when consent or a ready ad is unavailable.
  • Your control: Use the in-app ad privacy choices where available. You can purchase ad removal or use active SecureSets Pro to disable ads.

Google Play Billing and purchase verification

Google Play processes payment and purchase information under Google's terms. SecureSets offers one-time purchases and a renewing Pro subscription where available. Google Play manages subscription renewals and cancellation. Purchase access is tied to the Google Play account that owns the purchase; SecureSets checks purchases when it opens, when access is restored, and periodically while the app stays active. SecureSets does not receive or store payment-card details.

For Android purchase checks, SecureSets sends the Google Play purchase token and product ID over HTTPS to its purchase-verification service in Google Cloud's EU region (europe-west4, Netherlands). The service uses them temporarily to ask the Google Play Developer API whether the purchase is valid, active, refunded, or expired. It does not store purchase tokens in a database or write them to application logs. Google Cloud may process connection and operational metadata, such as the network address, request time and service diagnostics, to operate and secure the service; see Google Cloud's Privacy Notice. Workout routines, workout history and body profile details are not sent to this service.

Optional offline workout model

If you choose to install it, SecureSets downloads the approximately 491 MB Qwen2.5 0.5B Instruct model from Hugging Face. Hugging Face may receive ordinary connection metadata needed to serve the model file. SecureSets does not include your training preferences or routines in the download request.

After installation, the model processes your selected goal, training days, equipment, session length and excluded workout splits on your device. It does not use your workout history or account information. Prompts and generated plans stay on the device and are not sent to Qwen or Hugging Face. The model is optional and can be removed from Settings. The model is licensed under Apache 2.0; see the model page and license.

App updates

SecureSets uses Expo's update service to check for and download app updates. Update requests may include app version, runtime and device connection information. See Expo's Privacy Policy.

The app:

  • Does not use analytics SDKs
  • Does not use social media integrations
  • Uses an internet connection for ads, purchase checks, app updates, and optional model downloads

Children's Privacy

SecureSets is not specifically directed at children. Because advertising and consent requirements can differ by age and region, the store audience settings and AdMob child-directed treatment must be configured accurately before publication.

Deletion

You can delete local training data from Settings or by uninstalling the app. Uninstalling may remove local training data, subject to operating-system backup and restore settings.

There is no data on our servers to delete.

Data Retention

Data is retained on your device indefinitely until you choose to delete it or uninstall the app.

Your Rights

SecureSets does not maintain an account or server copy of your workout data. You can manage or delete local data from the device. For information processed by Google AdMob, use the available in-app ad choices or Google's privacy tools and policies.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

  • Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
  • Displaying a notice in the app (for significant changes)

Internet Permissions

SecureSets requires internet permission for advertising, Google Play purchase checks, app updates, and the optional offline model download. Workout records and body profile details are not sent to SecureSets servers. Google Play, AdMob, Expo and Hugging Face may receive information needed for the services described above. Workout preferences are processed locally when the offline model generates a plan.

Security

SecureSets stores workout information locally and does not operate a training-data server. The app does not add a separate database-encryption layer:

  • Keep your device and operating system up to date.
  • Use care when exporting or sharing a workout backup.
  • Review device backup and lock-screen settings.
